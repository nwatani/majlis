Content encryption (E2EE)—

        
Media is always DTLS-SRTP encrypted peer-to-peer. Admin commands are ECDSA-signed and verified by every client. When a room key exists, chat/whiteboard payloads are AES-256-GCM encrypted; the key lives only in the invite URL fragment and is never transmitted.
